Questions

Straight answers.

How much do I have to move my mouth?+
As little as you can while still forming the words. Someone next to you may notice your lips move; across a desk they won't.
Does it read my thoughts?+
No. It reads how your mouth moves. If your mouth doesn't move, it has nothing to read.
Why not just use a mic?+
You can, and a $30 lapel mic is quieter than your voice. This is for when quiet isn't quiet enough: a shared desk, a train, a library, a call you don't want overheard.
What's accuracy like?+
On my face, 20 command words: 9 in 10 right once it's on, about 7 in 10 straight after I take it off and put it back on, more after 30 seconds of calibration. The electrode demo was six phrases at 90.5%. These are floors, not a product. The first 50 units are for people who want to help close the gap, and you'll see the real numbers as they come in.
Will it work on my face?+
I don't know yet. That is exactly what this week's tests on other people are for, and I'll publish the numbers either way. If it doesn't work for you, you get your deposit back.
What does it work with?+
Mac first. It types into whatever app is open: Claude Code, Cursor, Slack, Gmail, WhatsApp, Notes.
Do I have to wear glasses?+
No. It hooks over your ear, or clips to your glasses if you wear them. The shape isn't final, and the first 50 decide it with me.
When does it ship?+
Estimated summer 2027. That's a good-faith estimate, not a promise. If it slips, I'll tell you the new estimate and you can cancel for a full refund.
How do refunds work?+
Cancel for any reason before final payment and you get the whole deposit back, to the card you paid with, within seven business days. Reply to your Stripe receipt or email me. I never charge the balance automatically.
Why a deposit now?+
Because the only honest signal that this should exist is whether people will pay for it. If fewer than 50 people do, I'll tell you, and refund everyone.
Is it a medical device?+
No. It makes no medical claims and treats nothing. It's an input device, like a keyboard, that you control with the movement of your mouth.
