Questions

Who is this for?

Anyone who understands speech and can still shape words with their mouth, but can't make sound: after laryngectomy, vocal cord paralysis, tracheostomy, or severe spasmodic dysphonia. If the muscles of the mouth are themselves weakened, it may not work, and I'll tell you honestly at the fitting.

How long does a fitting take?

About an hour the first time: placing the sensor, recording you mouthing a set of phrases, and testing. Then it improves every time you use it.

Is it a medical device?

No. It makes no medical claims and treats nothing. It is an input device, like a keyboard, that you control with your mouth instead of your fingers.
