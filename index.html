All questions →
How much do I have to move my mouth?+
As little as you can while still forming the words. Someone next to you may notice your lips move; across a desk they won't.
Does it read my thoughts?+
No. It reads how your mouth moves. If your mouth doesn't move, it has nothing to read.
Why not just use a mic?+
You can, and a $30 lapel mic is quieter than your voice. This is for when quiet isn't quiet enough: a shared desk, a train, a library, a call you don't want overheard.
What's accuracy like?+
On my face, 20 command words: 9 in 10 right once it's on, about 7 in 10 straight after I take it off and put it back on, more after 30 seconds of calibration. The electrode demo was six phrases at 90.5%. These are floors, not a product. The first 50 units are for people who want to help close the gap, and you'll see the real numbers as they come in.
Will it work on my face?+
I don't know yet. That is exactly what this week's tests on other people are for, and I'll publish the numbers either way. If it doesn't work for you, you get your deposit back.
